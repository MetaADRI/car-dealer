Who We Are

Our Story

Founded in 2003, AutoElite began as a small family-owned dealership in Lusaka with a vision to redefine the car buying experience. What started as a modest operation with just five employees has grown into one of Zambia's most trusted premium car dealerships.

Our journey has been guided by a simple philosophy: treat every customer like family and exceed their expectations at every turn. This commitment to excellence has earned us numerous industry awards and, more importantly, the trust of thousands of satisfied customers across Lusaka, the Copperbelt, and beyond.

Today, we continue to innovate and expand our services while staying true to our core values of integrity, transparency, and customer-first service.
